# F4 incident-routing sources

Verified 2026-08-20 against official pages. Re-check quarterly (next review 2026-11-20).

- Emergency: STS 112 service, https://sts.ro/ro/servicii/despre-112/
- Non-urgent fisheries complaints: ANPA reporting notice, https://www.anpa.ro/?p=1949. Published contacts: 0374 466 139, 0374 466 140, anpa@anpa.ro.
- Pollution: GNM official contact directory, https://www.gnm.ro/contact/. The app links to this directory rather than copying volatile county contacts. Verified central fallback: 021 326 89 70, gardamediu@gnm.ro.

Scope: read-only routing only. The app does not submit incident details, collect identity/GPS/media, or guarantee enforcement. 112 is shown for active or immediately dangerous events only. No national AJVPS, Jandarmerie, or ANADSPA incident hotline is published.
